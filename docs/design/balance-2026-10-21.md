# Balance sweep — ball flight + steering (2026-10-21)

The engine change that made shots and passes travel and stopped players
stacking (shared-core `04d9ad1`) changes outcomes, so it was tuned against a
reproducible sweep: `node scripts/balance-sweep.mjs [engineDir] [sims]`.
It runs fights through the real WASM engine in Node, set up and ticked the way
the game does (30 s of sim time, then up to 15 s of sudden-death OT; a tie
after OT is a home loss). Run it before and after any engine change.

## Result (1000 fights per matchup)

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| random mirror, home win | 47–50% | **50%** |
| scoreless games (mirror) | 4% | **5%** |
| top-cost five vs bottom-cost five, home win | 89–90% | **83%** |
| bunching (player pairs < 20 px, avg) | 0.78 | **0.00** |
| ball teleports (> 30 px in one tick) per s | 0.60 | **0.07** |
| avg score, top vs bottom | 5.2–2.0 | 3.6–1.2 |
| shots per game | ~6 | ~4.5 |

The remaining teleports are steals (the ball jumps ≤ 40 px to the defender);
the client's 120 ms hand-off ease covers them. Scoring is about a third lower
because flights take real time (about 11% of a round).

## What the tuning found

Each step was measured, not guessed:

1. **First cut: 72% / 39%, 17–24% scoreless.** 2.7 shots and 13.6 passes a
   game: separation walled off the drive (the on-ball defender stands 30 px
   ahead) and kept the carrier under pressure, which triggers passes.
2. **Carrier plays through contact (10 px):** shots 3.2–3.8, still low.
3. **Pass ping-pong:** the receiver's defender closed out during the flight,
   so every catch was pressured and passed on. Fix: the defense reacts to the
   catch, not the throw; 0.6 s catch-and-go; passes 700 px/s.
4. **Time budget** (share of ticks the ball is held / in a shot / in a pass /
   loose): before 81 / 0 / 0 / 19, after 69 / 8 / 3 / 21. Fixes: outlet pass
   after a make, quicker release (0.35 s + d/800), pass rate 0.45 × playmaking.
5. **Shot contest:** with the carrier pushing through at 10 px, his defender
   was pinned inside the 5 ft contest radius on every shot (penalty ~0.38 vs
   ~0.14 at the old ~30 px). Carrier contact 26 px restored accuracy and
   brought the mirror back to 50%.

## Open

- Scoring is lower than before. If rounds feel thin, the client-side levers
  are `SIM_SPEED` / `SIM_DURATION` in `CourtCanvas.vue` (more basketball per
  round), not the engine.
- Top vs bottom at 83% (was ~90%): talent still decides, slightly less
  absolutely. Worth watching in real runs.
